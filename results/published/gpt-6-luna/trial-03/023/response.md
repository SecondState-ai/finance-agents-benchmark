### Covenant and potential acceleration risk (separate from the net-debt calculation)

This loan has a **1.60x** net funded debt / trailing covenant EBITDA ceiling at 31-Dec-2025 (**Credit_agreement.pdf**, p. 1). Using $36.0m net funded debt and reported EBITDA of $21.466m from **Management_accounts_2025-12.xlsx**, sheet *2025-12 YTD*, the ratio is **1.677x**, above the limit. The certificate (**Compliance_certificate.pdf**, p. 3) instead uses $2.330m proposed add-backs to reach $23.796m EBITDA and 1.513x. The credit agreement allows nonrecurring implementation and settled-litigation costs with invoices/releases, but excludes forecast savings, compensation estimates and ordinary staff turnover. The $650,000 settlement is supported by **Settlement_and_release.pdf** (p. 1) and is described as fully settled with releases. The $900,000 ERP implementation amount appears in GL account **609000** / the year-to-date management accounts and is described in **Board_minutes_2025-12.docx**, but the underlying implementation invoices should be delivered/confirmed against the agreement's documentation condition. The board says eight employees received the $480,000 territory-review severance in 2025 after six employees received $360,000 in 2024; this repeated annual review is not clearly an eligible nonrecurring add-back and may be excluded as ordinary turnover/restructuring. The proposed $300,000 owner salary adjustment is a replacement-compensation estimate without a benchmarking report and should not be relied upon under the stated terms.

With only the $650,000 settlement adjustment, leverage is **1.628x**; with both the settlement and $900,000 implementation adjustment, leverage is **1.564x**, with $825,600 covenant headroom (1.60 × $23.016m less $36.0m). Thus the covenant outcome depends on whether at least $1.034m of the proposed adjustments is accepted/documented. **Bank_certificate_correspondence.eml** (13-Feb-2026) says the bank has not accepted the restructuring or owner-compensation add-backs and grants no waiver. This is a material lender/default/acceleration diligence point, not an extra debt balance that can be quantified from the room. Obtain the lender's written covenant calculation, acceptance of eligible add-backs and any waiver/consent; the supplied certificate alone does not establish bank acceptance.
